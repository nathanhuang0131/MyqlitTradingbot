# Aetherfall: Ashes of the Broken Crown — Story Bible v1

## Tone
Hybrid dark fantasy + political thriller + mythic prophecy, written with anime-drama emotional intensity.
Choices matter. Relationships matter. The world advances by Days.

## Core Mechanics That Drive Story
- Time moves: each Day has 5 Rounds. Spending time has consequences.
- World pressure: Diablo’s influence expands by day.
- Politics: the Human King can be assassinated (Grand Killer arc).
- Myth: special characters act in parallel to the player (ordinary route), causing ripple effects.
- Romance: AFFECTION/TRUST/BOND unlock duo skills and endings.

---

## World Setup
Centuries ago, Humans sealed the King of Diablo beneath Black Stone Ruins.
The seal was imperfect and fractured the law of magic. Elves blame humans for disturbing forbidden ruins.
Now the sky rains ash — the seal is breaking.

Diablo does not rise as chaos; he rises as a ruler who promises "order without weakness."
The kingdom fractures between stability and truth.

---

## Factions

### Humans (Playable)
A fragile kingdom. The court is full of spies, priests, and generals.
The throne is beloved — and surrounded.

### Elves
Proud and pragmatic. Enemy to Goblins/Demons, distrustful of Humans.
They will cooperate only if humans prove honor.

### Goblins
Allied to Diablo. Revenge-driven raiders and opportunists.

### Demons
Allied to Diablo. Not mindless; some are old nobles of a fallen realm.
They believe Diablo is restoration.

---

## Prophecy of the God Given
"When ash crowns the sky, a child of no lineage shall rise.
Blessed by no god, yet given all power.
If he conquers before the Crown falls, the world shall kneel in singular peace.
If not — fate shall devour itself."

This is the paradox route: God Given must conquer before Diablo dies.

## Special Characters (World Anchors)
These are legendary figures who exist in the world, with their own arcs.

1) King of Diablo — conqueror ideology; final boss for most routes.
Artifact: Crown of Ash Dominion.

2) Supreme Fighter — tragic hero who believes victory must be earned in single combat.
Artifact: Heavenbreak Halberd.
His death changes world morale.

3) World of Magician — sees the law of magic collapsing; fights for reality stability.
Artifact: Grimoire: World Spiral.

4) Light of Paladin — holy avatar trying to unite factions and cleanse corruption.
Artifact: Lumin Oathshield.

5) Grand Killer — political assassin; wants to kill the Human King to "save the nation."
Artifact: Night Verdict Daggers.
If he succeeds: civil war branch.

6) God Given — broken by design; can bend rules; must conquer world before Diablo is killed.
Artifact: God Given Sigil.
Lose if: Diablo dies first OR Supreme Fighter dies OR God Given dies.

## Ordinary Route (Player is not a special character)

### Act I — Rising Ash (Days 1–3)
- Tutorial town + rumors of ashfall
- First dungeon: Black Stone Ruins
- First companion recruitment
- First cameo of a special character (non-combat)
Decision axis introduced: stability (support crown) vs truth (expose corruption).

### Act II — Fractured Alliances (Days 4–7)
- Elf confrontation and forced cooperation encounter
- Goblin raids escalate; supply lines threatened
- Church reveals cracks: infiltration and forbidden relics
Grand Killer arc begins in the capital.

### Act III — Crimson Eclipse (Days 8–12)
- Supreme Fighter duel event
- Assassination attempt window opens
- Special characters collide indirectly through rumors and aftermaths
Romance turning points and betrayals can occur based on TRUST.

### Act IV — Throne of Diablo (Days 13+)
- Multi-route approach to Diablo’s realm: Holy, Arcane, Shadow (assassin)
- Artifact ownership and alliances shape outcomes
Final confrontation: Diablo and/or ideology consequences.

## Special Routes (Player is a special character)
- King of Diablo route: conquer capitals, crush resistance, enforce Ash Dominion.
- God Given route: conquer world before Diablo dies; paranoia + clock pressure.
- Supreme Fighter route: duel-centered tragedy; politics hunts you.
- World of Magician route: prevent magic-law collapse after Diablo.
- Light of Paladin route: unity and sacrifice; cleanse corruption.
- Grand Killer route: political thriller; stopping Diablo may still cost the crown.

## Ending Matrix (v1)
1) Hero of Dawn — Diablo defeated, king lives, alliance formed.
2) Broken Crown — Diablo defeated, king dead → civil war.
3) Assassin’s Peace — king killed by Grand Killer, Diablo defeated → authoritarian stability.
4) Ash Dominion — Diablo conquers.
5) God Given Overlord — God Given conquers before Diablo dies.
6) Crimson Tragedy — Supreme Fighter dies; victory at massive cost.
7) Forbidden Collapse — Diablo defeated but magic ruptures (magician failure).
8) Love in Ruins — romance decides sacrifice/survival.
